# Project Description

This document provides a high-level overview of the two main portals (tabs) within the application: the **Candidate** page and the **Employee/Employer** page. 

## 1. Candidate Page
The Candidate portal is designed for job seekers, students, and professionals looking to enhance their skills and find career opportunities. Key features of this tab include:

- **Candidate Dashboard:** A centralized hub for users to track their applications, upcoming events, and overall progress.
- **Career & Opportunities:** Sections dedicated to exploring job openings (on-campus and off-campus), internships, and placement drives.
- **Skill Development & Practice:** Includes an integrated Coding IDE, Aptitude Tests, Case Studies, and a comprehensive Learning platform to hone technical and soft skills.
- **AI & Resume Tools:** Features like ResumeAI and ResumeBuilder to help candidates create industry-standard resumes.
- **Assessments & Profiles:** Tools for taking various assessments (OA Platform, AI Interview Simulator) and maintaining professional profiles (e.g., CodolioProfile, Portfolio).
- **Community & Communication:** Forums, GD Topics, Alumni Network, and Mail IQ for networking and communicating with peers and recruiters.

## 2. Employee / Employer Page
The Employee (Employer) portal is tailored for recruiters, hiring managers, and HR professionals to manage the entire recruitment lifecycle. Key features of this tab include:

- **Employer Dashboard:** A comprehensive overview of recruitment metrics, active roles, and recent activities.
- **Talent & Pipeline Management:** Tools for discovering talent, viewing detailed candidate profiles, and efficiently moving candidates through the hiring pipeline.
- **Roles Management:** Allows employers to post, edit, and track job openings and roles within the organization.
- **Interview Scheduling:** A dedicated scheduler for coordinating and managing candidate interviews seamlessly.
- **Workforce & Insights:** Analytics and workforce planning features to provide actionable insights into hiring trends and overall workforce dynamics.
- **Employer Branding:** Sections for managing the company's brand image and institutional or corporate partnerships.
